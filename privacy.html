Hire Me

Privacy policy

Effective 23 October 2026

Hire Me is a personal job-search app. It runs on its owner's Windows PC and is used by that one person only. This policy explains what Hire Me does with data from the owner's Google account.

What Hire Me accesses

With the owner's permission, Hire Me uses read-only access to Gmail (the gmail.readonly scope). It looks only for job-alert emails from job boards, such as LinkedIn, Indeed, Jobstreet and Bayt.

Hire Me will ask separately before using any further access. Planned features that would need it are sending a daily job digest to the owner's own address and adding interviews to Google Calendar. Until then, it has no access to them.

How it uses that data

  • From each job-alert email it takes the job details the email lists: title, company, location, pay and link.
  • It keeps those job details and each email's message ID, so the same email isn't read twice.
  • It does not keep the full text of emails, and it ignores emails that aren't job alerts.
  • The data is used only to show jobs on the owner's own board.

Where it is stored

Everything stays on the owner's PC, in a local database and in the app's own folder. That includes the job details and the Google sign-in token. Hire Me has no server, no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

Sharing

Google user data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI models. It is not shared with anyone, with one exception:

When the owner asks Hire Me to draft a tailored resume, a cover letter or interview notes for a job, that job's details are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to write the draft. The job's details may have come from an alert email. Nothing else from Gmail is sent.

Hire Me's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

  • Revoke Hire Me's access at any time from your Google account's third-party access page.
  • Deleting the app's local database and its HireMe folder in AppData removes everything it stored.

Changes and contact

If Hire Me starts using Google data in a new way, this page is updated first and the date above changes. Questions go to the owner through harumastudiodev on GitHub.
