Hire Me

A personal job-search app that runs on one Windows PC.

Hire Me gathers job listings onto a Kanban board, points out what matters about each one, and helps its owner apply. It is a private tool built for, and used by, a single person. It is not a public service and is not offered for download.

What it does

  • Collects public job listings from job boards that allow it, and reads the job-alert emails those boards send to its owner's Gmail.
  • Shows each job on a board with columns from Found to Closed, with pay converted to pesos per month.
  • Points out things worth knowing, such as pay below a set floor, location, or who may apply. It never hides a job.
  • Helps draft tailored resumes and cover letters, which stay drafts until reviewed.

Google account access

Hire Me asks for read-only access to its owner's Gmail so it can read job-alert emails. Everything it keeps stays on that PC. The privacy policy explains exactly what is read, kept and shared.
